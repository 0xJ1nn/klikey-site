One-paragraph description

KliKey is a keyboard app by JinUltimate. It gives every key its own sound, its own animation and its own haptic, across 25 themes, three of which turn typing into music. It ships 73 languages on native key layouts, and collects no data at all. The free tier never expires.

What makes it different

Sound

28 voices, each with its own haptic. Voice packs include Boop, Cartoon and Machine. Mechanical themes reproduce individual switch sounds, including the tick of a key spring when you lift your finger. The sound lab has sliders for volume, click, pitch, haptics and key travel.

Music

Lo-Fi Room, Kalimba and Spectrum turn every key into a note from a moving chord progression, so whatever you type comes out sounding like music. Letters play notes, the space bar holds the bass, and return plays the chord. The progression moves underneath you as you type.

Haptics

28 haptic voices matched per theme. Profiles include thock, click, pop and rumble. The space bar and return hit noticeably harder than the rest of the row. Mechanical themes add a release tick when you lift your finger.

Themes

25 themes across five categories: clean, cartoony, modern, sci-fi and music. Porcelain, Tok, Titanium, Vector HUD, Magma and Ninja are among them, and Ninja is also a theme you can wear on its own. Every theme has its own background (Soft glow, Dot grid, Solid), its own accent colour, and its own spacebar effect that can be switched off per theme in Settings. 13 press effects included. Locked themes carry a PRO badge, and every one of them can be tried before you buy it.

Tok

There is someone in your spacebar. Tok follows your cursor, watches each key, and winces when you hit delete. He is happy when you type fast, jumps when you hit send, and falls asleep if you stop for seven seconds. He has moods: watching, in the flow, sent, delete, napping, ninja mode. Cream for the cute crowd, Night for everyone else, and you can pick one per theme or set him globally.

Ninja and Incognito

Two separate switches, both free. Ninja goes over any theme: blank keys, no previews, silent. Incognito means nothing is learned, and there is no clipboard or emoji history. The keyboard performs no network I/O, and the Play data safety page declares no data collected and no data shared with third parties. Signing in is optional, and learned words sync only to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive.

Languages

73 languages, each on its own key layout rather than a translated English one. Thai, Khmer and Armenian get five rows. Hindi joins its vowels. Long-press accents, punctuation that follows the language, predictions and next-word suggestions in every one of them, and swipe typing in the 40 where it has been measured to hold up. Thai, Lao and Khmer read typed words out loud from their own dictionaries.

Tools

Number row, numpad, clipboard with pins, a cursor pad, one-handed and split layouts, emoji and symbols that chime, kaomoji, fancy text, and emoji search above the panel. The 4-dot key opens the options panel, and the space bar slides the cursor and flashes your language.
