Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

KliKey works on your phone. There is no KliKey server: what you type is never sent to us, never stored on a server, and never used to build a profile. No analytics, no advertising ID, no ads.

What the keyboard can see

Like any keyboard, KliKey sees the text in the field you are typing into, so it can show suggestions and corrections. That text is used on your device only, and it is discarded as you go. Nothing is logged.

What is stored on your phone

If learning is on, KliKey keeps a small list of the words and word pairs you actually use, so typing gets better. It is kept in the app's private storage, excluded from cloud backups, and it never leaves your phone unless you turn on sync (below). Learning is skipped entirely for passwords, number pads, e-mail and URL fields, any app that asks for no suggestions, and while Incognito is on.

Learned-words sync (optional, free, off by default)

If you choose Google Drive and tap Connect Google Drive, Google asks you to let KliKey use its own hidden folder in your Drive. KliKey then keeps a copy of your learned words — nothing else — in that folder, so they come back on another phone connected to the same Drive.

  • KliKey runs no server and never receives this data. It stays in your Google account.
  • The folder is KliKey's private app folder: it is invisible in the Drive interface and other apps cannot open it. Google, which hosts it, can.
  • The copy is encrypted, but the key is stored in the same folder, so what protects it is your Google account, not the encryption. Keep your Google account secure.
  • What you type is never synced — only the word list the keyboard learned.
  • Disconnecting Drive keeps your words on the phone. Erase learned words deletes the local list and the Drive copy. You can also revoke access at any time in your Google Account under Data & privacy → Third-party apps.

Ninja & Incognito

Ninja hides what is on the keys: blank keys, no previews, no sound, and a panic swipe that wipes the draft. Incognito remembers nothing: no learned words, no clipboard history, no recent emoji. Incognito can turn on by itself in password, PIN, card, one-time-code and private fields; Ninja only ever turns on when you turn it on. Privacy is never a paid feature.

Clipboard and recents

If you copy text, KliKey may offer it in its clipboard panel, on this device only. Incognito pauses that, and you can clear it any time in Settings.

Purchases

KliKey Pro is billed by Google Play (Android) or the App Store (iOS). We never see your payment details. The store tells us only whether a purchase is active, so Pro can be unlocked.

Permissions KliKey asks for

  • Vibration — the haptics every key has.
  • Network — used only by the optional sync, and only when you have connected Google Drive. With sync off, KliKey itself never touches the network (purchases go through the store app).
  • Full Access (iOS) — only so the keyboard can play sound, use haptics, and share settings with the app.

Your control

Settings → Erase learned words deletes everything KliKey has learned, permanently, including the synced copy. Turning the keyboard off or uninstalling the app removes the rest.

Children

KliKey is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If this policy changes we will show the new version in the app and update the date above.
